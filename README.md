# 陈平人性谋略 (Chen Ping Strategic Psychology)

将陈平——中国历史上最懂人性的顶级谋士——的核心思维模式提炼为可复用的AI指南和可加载技能。让你在面对复杂人际局面时，拥有一个从人性底层出发的战略大脑。

## The Problem

Most people navigate workplace politics, competition, and influence through intuition, trial-and-error, or moral platitudes. They:
- Confront opponents directly instead of eliminating their motives
- Appeal to fairness when everyone is driven by self-interest
- Try to prove their value when they should be managing perception
- Fight human nature instead of using it

## The Solution

Chen Ping (陈平), chief strategist to Emperor Liu Bang (Han Dynasty, ~200 BC), solved the hardest political and military problems of his era not through force, but through deep insight into human nature. His methods were so effective that Sima Qian (the greatest Chinese historian) wrote: "He frequently produced unexpected stratagems, rescued the state from turmoil, and restored the nation from disaster."

This repository distills his approach into:

| Artifact | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **10 Actionable Principles** | Each grounded in historical events, with modern business + career applications |
| **CLAUDE.md** | Behavioral guidelines that make Claude Code embody Chen Ping's strategic thinking |
| **EXAMPLES.md** | Before/after scenarios: conventional approach vs. Chen Ping-guided approach |
| **info.md** | Deep analysis of his thinking system with historical evidence |

## The 10 Principles

| # | Principle | Core Idea |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 利益驱动 (Interest-Driven) | Everyone moves toward benefit and away from harm — find the interest, find the leverage |
| 2 | 动机消除 (Motive Elimination) | Don't fight the person — remove their reason to fight you |
| 3 | 猜忌放大 (Suspicion Amplification) | Leaders fear internal betrayal — feed what they already suspect |
| 4 | 对比落差 (Contrast Engineering) | Control the reference point, control the conclusion |
| 5 | 侥幸心理 (Wishful Thinking) | People default to the most comforting interpretation of ambiguity |
| 6 | 枕边风 (Side Door) | Influence through trusted intimates, not direct assault |
| 7 | 妥协隐忍 (Strategic Patience) | Survive, wait, strike when the balance shifts |
| 8 | 自污避祸 (Deflect Through Flaws) | Perfection terrifies power — visible weakness makes you safe |
| 9 | 以退为进 (Yield to Advance) | Step back at the peak to avoid being pushed off |
| 10 | 结果导向 (Results Over Methods) | The objective justifies the approach — but know the cost |

## How to Know It's Working

Chen Ping's framework is working if:
- You identify hidden interests behind stated positions
- You design situations where others voluntarily choose your preferred outcome
- You resolve conflicts by eliminating motives rather than escalating force
- You see power dynamics clearly and navigate them without becoming a target

## When NOT to Use

These principles are designed for **high-stakes situations** where human dynamics are the critical variable. Do not apply them to:
- Simple, transparent interactions where goodwill is sufficient
- Relationships built on trust that would be damaged by strategic calculation
- Trivial disputes where the moral cost exceeds the benefit

**Tradeoff:** These principles bias toward effectiveness over purity. Chen Ping himself warned that dark strategies carry karmic cost — use them when the objective is worthy.
