# CONTEXT.md

Domain glossary for **陈平 (Chen Ping) — Strategic Psychology** — canonical terms used throughout this repository.

## Core Concepts

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **人性本利 (Interest-Driven Human Nature)** | The foundational belief that all human behavior is driven by self-interest — the pursuit of benefit and avoidance of harm. Not a moral judgment, but an operational truth. Chen Ping's entire strategic system rests on this axiom. |
| **动机消除 (Motive Elimination)** | Removing the reason someone would act against you, rather than confronting the person directly. If there's no profit in attacking you, the attack never materializes. Distinct from deterrence — deterrence raises the cost; elimination removes the incentive entirely. |
| **猜忌放大 (Suspicion Amplification)** | Exploiting the inherent insecurity of people in power by feeding their existing paranoia. The key insight: you don't need to fabricate evidence — you only need to say what the target already fears might be true. |
| **对比落差 (Contrast Gap)** | Manipulating perception by creating stark contrasts in treatment, framing, or options. Human judgment is relative, not absolute — a mediocre option looks excellent after a terrible one, and vice versa. The mechanism is perceptual framing, not direct harm. |
| **侥幸心理 (Wishful Thinking)** | The universal tendency to choose the most optimistic interpretation of an ambiguous situation. When facing uncertain risk, people default to "it'll be fine" rather than preparing for the worst. |
| **信息差 (Information Asymmetry)** | Controlling what each party knows and doesn't know. The master lever of manipulation — by selectively revealing or concealing information, you control the reality each person perceives and therefore the decisions they make. Underpins Principle 3 (猜忌放大), Principle 4 (对比落差), and Step 4 of the decision framework. |
| **枕边风 (Pillow-Talk Channel)** | Influencing a decision-maker through the person they trust most intimately — spouse, confidant, secretary — rather than through direct persuasion. Bypasses rational defenses by targeting emotional proximity. |
| **势 (Strategic Momentum / The Current)** | The shape and direction of power at a given moment. A fundamental Chinese strategic concept: power has momentum like water has current. You either ride it (顺势而为) or get swept away by it. Principles 7 (妥协隐忍), 8 (自污避祸), and 9 (以退为进) are all about reading 势 and positioning relative to it — not passive waiting, active alignment with the flow of power. |
| **不战而屈人之兵 (Win Without Fighting)** | The ideal outcome of Chen Ping's entire system — achieving your objective without visible conflict. The opponent chooses your preferred path voluntarily, never realizing they were guided there. This is the north star: every principle aims to make fighting unnecessary. |
| **妥协隐忍 (Compromise & Forbearance)** | Strategic retreat when outmatched. Not cowardice — preserving oneself to win later. The key distinction: compromise on tactics, never on the ultimate objective. |
| **自污避祸 (Self-Defilement)** | Deliberately displaying weakness, vice, or incompetence to appear non-threatening to those in power. A person with visible flaws cannot be a conspirator — perfection itself is suspicious. |
| **以退为进 (Retreat to Advance)** | Voluntarily yielding position or credit to avoid the danger of having too much power. When you've risen too high, the only safe direction is one step back. |
| **阴谋 (Dark Strategy)** | Chen Ping's own term for his methods — strategies that operate through deception, manipulation of perception, and exploitation of human weakness. He himself acknowledged the karmic cost: "吾多阴谋，是道家之所禁" (My many dark strategies are what the Dao forbids). |

## Decision Framework

Chen Ping's approach to any strategic problem follows a consistent 5-step pipeline. The 10 principles are the tactical expressions of these 5 steps:

| Step | Question | Purpose | Principles |
|------|----------|---------|------------|
| **1. Map Interests** | Who wants what? Who gains from each outcome? | Identify real drivers behind stated positions | Principle 1: 利益驱动 |
| **2. Find the Weakness** | What does each person fear? What do they desire most? | Locate leverage — greed, fear, jealousy, pride, insecurity | Principle 3: 猜忌放大, Principle 5: 侥幸心理 |
| **3. Create the Situation** | How can I arrange circumstances so their self-interest leads them to my desired outcome? | Design the environment; don't issue commands | Principle 2: 动机消除, Principle 4: 对比落差 |
| **4. Cut Information** | What should each person know and not know? | Control perception to control decision | Principle 6: 枕边风 |
| **5. Preserve Yourself** | What's the exit strategy? Am I exposed? | Never be the visible instigator; survive to win later | Principle 7: 妥协隐忍, Principle 8: 自污避祸, Principle 9: 以退为进 |

**Meta-principle:** Principle 10 (结果导向) governs the entire pipeline — evaluate every step by whether it works, but remain aware of the cumulative moral cost.
